About Lexora

Lexora is an AI-based content analysis system built to assist in identifying potentially harmful or suspicious social-media content, including language that may be abusive, threatening, or antinational in nature. It is intended as a support tool for review workflows rather than an automatic or final decision-maker.

Like any language model, Lexora's output can be incomplete or mistaken, and results should be treated as a starting signal for human judgement, not a guarantee of accuracy.
