Project details

Lexora — AI-powered content analysis

Lexora is an AI-based content analysis system designed to identify abusive, threatening, antinational, and other potentially harmful content from textual social-media data. It is built as a support tool for review workflows, not a fully automated or guaranteed detection system.

Project overview

What it is

Lexora is an AI-based model that reads social-media text and flags patterns commonly associated with abusive, threatening, or antinational language.

Why it was built

Harmful content online is difficult to catch manually at scale. Lexora explores how automated analysis can give reviewers a faster starting point.

Where it helps

It's designed to support community moderation, research, and digital-safety workflows — anywhere a first pass over text content would save time.

Problem statement

The greatest challenge in digital moderation is not finding harmful words—it is recognizing harmful intent hidden behind ordinary words. Millions of comments and posts can contain abuse, threats, hate, incitement, anti-national content, or misinformation, yet traditional keyword-based systems struggle with context, slang, sarcasm, coded expressions, and the actual target of a message. At the same time, aggressive but lawful criticism can be incorrectly treated as harmful content, creating a serious risk of unfair moderation. What is needed is a system that can determine what the message means, whom it targets, why it is harmful, and how serious the risk is, while keeping humans responsible for the final action.
